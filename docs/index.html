Chrome extension for claude.ai

Every question you asked,
one click away

Chat Outline adds a table of contents to claude.ai. Every question from the chat sits in one panel. Click one and you're there, even in chats too long for claude.ai to load at once.

Download for Chrome Watch it work

Free and open source. No account, no tracking.

What it does

Small panel. Long chats stop being a scroll marathon

Jump to any question

Click a question and the chat scrolls straight to it. No more dragging the scrollbar and squinting.

Knows where you are

The question you're reading stays highlighted as you scroll, so the panel doubles as a map.

⌘⇧↑↓

Next question, one key

Cmd+Shift+↑ and ↓ step through your questions, panel open or not. From the middle of a long answer, up goes back to its question first.

Continue where you left off

Picks up where you left off

claude.ai always opens a chat at the end. Come back to a long one and it offers to take you to the question you were reading.

i love claude

Search your questions

Type a word and the list narrows to the questions that contain it. Handy when the chat has two hundred of them.

Star what matters

Star the questions you'll come back to and show only those. Stars stay on your machine.

⌘⇧O

Panel on a shortcut

Cmd+Shift+O shows or hides the panel. Arrow keys move through the list, Cmd+↑/↓ to its first and last question.

Looks like it belongs

Same fonts and colors as claude.ai, in light and dark. It follows whatever theme claude.ai is in.

Long chats

Reaches questions claude.ai hasn't loaded yet

In a long chat, claude.ai only keeps the latest messages on the page and loads older ones as you scroll up. Outline tools that read the page can only list what's there, so the start of the chat goes missing.

Chat Outline reads the conversation the same way claude.ai does, so the list is complete from the first message. Click a question from an hour ago and it loads the earlier messages and takes you there.

Nothing leaves your browser

It runs only on claude.ai and reads the open chat through claude.ai's own API, signed in as you. There's no server, no analytics, no account. Stars, settings and a cache of question texts stay in Chrome's local storage. The code is on GitHub if you want to check.

Install

Up and running in a minute

  1. 1

    Download

    Get claude-chat-outline.zip and unzip it

  2. 2

    Turn on Developer mode

    Open chrome://extensions and flip the switch in the top right corner

  3. 3

    Load it

    Click Load unpacked, pick the unzipped folder and open any chat on claude.ai

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers (114+)
